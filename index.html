Services

Services that we provide to maintain your quality smile

Extraction

Safe and gentle removal of damaged, decayed, or problematic teeth to relieve pain, prevent infection, and protect your overall oral health. Our experienced dentists ensure a comfortable procedure with personalized aftercare for a smooth recovery.

Scaling and Polishing

Scaling and polishing removes plaque, tartar (calculus), and surface stains to keep your teeth clean and healthy. It helps prevent bad breath, gum disease, and plaque build-up while leaving your teeth smoother and brighter.

Filling

A tooth filling is a quick and effective treatment that repairs cavities and minor tooth damage. It restores the tooth's strength, function, and natural appearance while helping prevent further decay and protecting your long-term oral health.

Dentures

Dentures are custom-made removable replacements for missing teeth that restore your smile, improve chewing and speaking, and support facial structure. Whether you need full or partial dentures, modern designs provide a comfortable, natural-looking solution to help you regain confidence and enjoy everyday life.

Braces and Aligners

Achieve a healthier, straighter smile with our orthodontic treatments. We offer both traditional braces and clear aligners to gently correct crooked teeth, gaps, overcrowding, and bite issues. Our personalized treatment plans are designed to give you comfortable care and long-lasting results, helping you smile with confidence.

Teeth Whitening

Brighten your smile with our safe and effective professional teeth whitening treatments. We use advanced whitening techniques to remove stains and discoloration caused by coffee, tea, smoking, and everyday wear, leaving your teeth several shades whiter. Enjoy a brighter, more confident smile with results that are fast, comfortable, and long-lasting.
